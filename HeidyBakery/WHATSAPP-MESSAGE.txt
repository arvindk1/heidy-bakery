Hi Heidy! Here's the Heidy Bakery Installer.pkg update.

Before installing:
- Save a full backup in the app first (Settings -> Save full backup).

To install:
1. Quit Heidy Bakery if it's open (Command + Q).
2. Tap the Heidy Bakery Installer.pkg file above.
3. A preview will pop up - tap Open with Installer.
4. Click Continue, then Install.
5. Enter your Mac password (or use Touch ID) if asked.
6. Wait for "Installation was successful," then close the installer.
7. Open your Applications folder and launch Heidy Bakery from there (not an old Dock icon).

What's new this time:
- Recipes -> Import recipe workbooks now reads your original Cake, Bread, Cookie & Others, and Cloud Chiffon files directly - you can select all four at once.
- About 53 recipes come in without selling prices - please check and set those before relying on them.
- Some recipe costs in the app may look higher than the spreadsheet's totals - a few rows (mostly stickers) were left out of the old sheet totals.
- Recipes marked HS need you to confirm which price channel they use before their prices are set.

Let me know if anything looks off or the installer doesn't open!
