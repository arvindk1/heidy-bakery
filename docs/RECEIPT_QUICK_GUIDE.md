# Heidy's Bakery — Receipt Quick Guide

> **Snap on your iPhone. Review on your Mac.**

---

## Set Up Once (On Your Mac)

1. Open **Heidy's Bakery**.
2. Go to **Settings → Choose folder**.
3. Select the folder used by your iPhone shortcut:
   ```
   iCloud Drive > Shortcuts > All new receipts
   ```

---

## The 4-Step Everyday Routine

### Step 1: Take the Photo (iPhone)
- On your iPhone, tap the shortcut **Save Bakery Receipt**.
- Take a clear, well-lit photo of the receipt.
- iCloud automatically transfers the file to your Mac in the background.

### Step 2: Bring It Into the App (Mac)
- In the Mac app, go to **Receipts** and click **Check receipt folder**.
- The app automatically imports any new files, skips duplicates, and reads the text on your Mac.

### Step 3: Check the Purchase Details (Mac)
- Select the receipt from the list on the left.
- Confirm the **Retailer** name and the **Purchase date**.
- Click **Find candidate lines**.
- Click **Edit** on each purchase to confirm:
  - The matched **ingredient**
  - The **paid price**
  - The **package quantity** and **unit**
- *Example*: For 60 eggs, enter quantity `60` and unit `each`.
- *Exclude*: Check the box to exclude personal items, snacks, or refunds.
- Saved store matches will be remembered for future receipts.

### Step 4: Approve the Updates (Mac)
- Once the details look right, click **Approve price updates** and confirm.
- Ingredient costs and suggested prices update automatically.

---

## Your Selling Prices Stay Under Your Control
The app calculates suggested prices based on your markups, but it **never changes your selling prices for you**.

---

## If You Get Stuck

- **No receipt showing up?**
  Check that the photo has finished uploading in Finder under `iCloud Drive > Shortcuts > All new receipts`, then click **Check receipt folder** again.
- **Item not found or text unreadable?**
  Click **Add purchase** to type the line manually from the receipt. Click **Open original** to zoom in on the receipt in Preview.
- **Made a mistake?**
  Go to **Settings → Undo last saved change** to revert immediately.
- **Regular Backups**:
  Save a full backup in **Settings → Save full backup** once a week to iCloud Drive or an external drive.
